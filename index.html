The current app processes your gallery and diary on your device. Lumi does not operate an account service or upload your media or diary to a developer-operated server. Sharing and backups happen when you choose them.

About Lumi

This policy applies to the Android app Lumi Gallery: Photos & Video, also referred to as Lumi Gallery or Lumi, and this privacy-policy website. The current Android application ID is com.gallery.app.

“We” and “us” mean the developer of Lumi Gallery, reachable at friendlink00@gmail.com. This policy describes the current app, including local Premium-preview features. It does not describe advertising or payment services that have not yet been added.

Data used on your device

Lumi accesses and stores information needed for the features you use:

  • Photos, videos and audio: media you allow Lumi to access; thumbnails and video previews; filenames, folders, media identifiers, size, type, duration and available dates. These support browsing, filtering, playback, editing, file management and exports.
  • Media location metadata: existing GPS information embedded in photos, where available and permitted, to support location-related organization and memories. Lumi may also use places you enter yourself. The current app does not request live device-location permission or track your movements.
  • Diary and journeys: titles, reflections, per-media writing, mood selections, places, dates, day headings, selected fonts, themes and references to media. This information is saved locally.
  • Editing projects and recordings: editing choices, text, overlays, selected music, temporary processing files and voice-over recordings made when you start recording. Exports are written to the location provided by the app or chosen through Android.
  • Preferences and privacy settings: favourites, sorting, appearance, hidden-media identifiers, local lock configuration and available failed-unlock history. The local Premium-preview preference is not proof of a purchase.
  • Share history: when enabled or recorded by the sharing feature, the selected destination app’s name/package, media reference and time. A recorded share means the hand-off was initiated; it does not prove delivery or identify a recipient.
  • Local technical information: device capabilities, storage information and diagnostic records needed for playback, exports and troubleshooting. The current app has no developer-operated analytics or crash-upload service.

We do not sell your personal information or use your photos, diary entries or recordings for advertising. The current app does not send this content to an AI service.

Sharing, exports and backups

When you share

If you choose to share a photo, video, export or other file, Android passes the selected content to the app or destination you choose. That recipient handles the content under its own privacy policy. Media may contain metadata, including location; review the content and metadata before sharing it.

Diary backups

Back up journal writes a diary backup through Android’s document picker to the destination you choose. A cloud-storage provider may receive that file if you choose its location. The backup contains diary text, metadata and media references; it is not a complete copy of your original photo/video library and is not encrypted by Lumi. Protect the backup and delete unwanted copies from their storage locations. Restore reads the backup you select.

Android backup and device transfer

Android or your device provider may back up or transfer app data according to the device’s backup settings and platform behavior. This is separate from a Lumi-operated cloud service. Manage those backups through your device and provider settings.

Hidden media and security

Lumi uses Android’s app-storage protections for local app data. PIN and pattern credentials are stored as salted hashes rather than readable PINs or patterns. Optional biometric checks are performed by Android. Available failed-unlock history records the attempt time and method; Lumi does not take an intruder photograph.

Hidden is not an encrypted vault. Hiding within Lumi filters the item from Lumi’s ordinary gallery without hiding it from other apps. Hide everywhere, when used, moves files into hidden shared-storage locations or marks folders to exclude them from ordinary gallery scanning; it does not encrypt the files. File managers, connected computers or other software with sufficient storage access may still find them.

Uninstalling Lumi does not automatically restore files hidden in shared storage. Restore or unhide important media before uninstalling, and keep independent backups. No storage or authentication method can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains for as long as it is needed for the features you use, until you delete it, or until it is removed by Android’s app-data management.

  • Diary: delete entries or edit their text and moods through Diary. Removing a diary entry does not delete its original photos or videos.
  • Media and exports: use the available delete, Recently deleted, file-manager or Android controls. Retention and recovery depend on the operation and Android version. Files you copied, exported or shared can remain in their separate destinations.
  • Share history: use the available history controls to remove recorded shares. This does not remove a copy held by a recipient.
  • App data: clearing Lumi’s storage in Android Settings or uninstalling normally removes app-private diary data, preferences and caches. It does not remove original media, exports or hidden files in shared storage, or external backups. Device or provider backups may retain their own copies.
  • Permissions: revoke access in Android Settings. Revoking permission limits future access but does not automatically delete data already saved locally.

There is no Lumi account to delete and no developer-held copy of your gallery or diary that we can remotely erase. Children and families

A child’s photos or writing stored on a device are handled by the local features described above. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.
